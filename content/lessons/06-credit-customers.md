## Give credit with rules, not feelings
id: credit-01
triggers: many_debtors
topics: credit, debt, deni, debtors

Selling on credit can keep loyal customers, but unpaid debts are the number one cash killer for small shops. Set simple rules and apply them to everyone:

• Only regular customers you know well.
• A limit per person (for example, no more than one week of their usual buying).
• No new credit until the old balance is paid.
• Record it immediately: "sold 2 sugar to Mary on credit".

I keep the credit book for you. Send *debtors* any time.

**Try this:** Write your credit rules on a paper at the counter so customers see them.

- C) Give double so they come back
**Why:** Stacking debt on debt makes it harder to collect either.

## Collecting what you are owed, politely
id: credit-02
triggers: many_debtors
topics: collect, reminder, chase, collection

Most customers pay. They just need a reminder at the right time. The trick is to be friendly, specific and consistent:

1. Remind on the agreed day: name, amount, and a simple way to pay (your mobile money number or till).
2. If late, remind again after a few days with a clear date.
3. For long-overdue debts, offer a payment plan: small regular amounts are better than nothing.

Send *remind* and I will prepare polite messages you can forward with one tap.
