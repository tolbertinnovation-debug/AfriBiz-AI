## Profit is not the same as cash
id: cash-01
triggers: many_debtors, losing_money
topics: profit, cash, cash flow

You can make a profit and still have no money. How? Sales on credit haven't come in yet, and money spent on stock is sitting on the shelf.

*Profit* = sales − cost of goods sold − expenses.
*Cash* = the money you actually have in hand and on your phone.

Your report shows both: "Profit" tells you if the business is worth running; "Cash in / Cash out" tells you if you can pay tomorrow's bills.

**Try this:** Send *report week* and compare your profit with your net cash. Which is bigger, and why?

**Quiz:** You sold 10,000 this week, but 4,000 was on credit. Your costs were 7,000 paid in cash. What happened to your cash?
- A) It went down by 1,000 *
- B) It went up by 3,000
- C) No change
**Why:** You collected only 6,000 but paid 7,000, so cash fell by 1,000, even though profit was +3,000.

## Plan for slow weeks
id: cash-02
triggers: losing_money
topics: plan, budget, slow season, school fees

Every business has rhythms: month-end is busy when people get paid, school-fee months are slow, rainy season changes what sells. Write down your big payments that are coming up (rent, school fees, licence, stock for a festive season).

Each week, set aside a small amount for those payments, before you spend on anything else. A separate savings wallet or a chama/susu contribution works well.

**Try this:** List your three biggest payments in the next three months and how much to save weekly for each.

**Quiz:** Rent of 9,000 is due in 3 months. How much should you set aside each week (about 12 weeks)?
- A) 750 *
- B) 3,000
- C) Nothing until the month it is due
**Why:** 9,000 ÷ 12 weeks = 750 per week. Small weekly saving avoids a crisis on rent day.
