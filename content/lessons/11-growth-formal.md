## Why register your business
id: growth-01
topics: register, registration, formal, license, tax, kra, cac, gra, ura

Registering (for example a business name with the Business Registration Service in Kenya, CAC in Nigeria, the Office of the Registrar of Companies in Ghana, or URSB in Uganda) can open doors: supplier credit, government and NGO tenders, bank accounts and larger loans in the business's name.

It also brings duties: tax registration, simple returns, and some licences. Many countries have simplified small-business tax regimes based on turnover. Good records (like the ones you keep here) make these easy.

**Try this:** Find out the cost and steps to register a business name in your country, and write down one opportunity it would open for you.

**Quiz:** Which is a common benefit of registering your business?
- A) You never pay any tax
- B) Access to contracts, bank accounts and loans in the business's name *
- C) Customers must buy from you
**Why:** Registration builds trust with banks, suppliers and big buyers.

## Know when to grow
id: growth-02
topics: grow, expand, second shop, employ

Signs you are ready to grow: steady profit for several months, customers asking for items you don't have, and records that show where the money comes from.

Grow in small, tested steps: add one new product line before opening a second shop. Hire a helper only when the extra sales clearly cover their wage. Check your numbers (*report month*) after every change.

**Try this:** Pick one small growth step and decide how you will measure whether it worked after one month.

- C) Start stocking a few gas cylinders and track the sales *
**Why:** A small test with records shows whether the new line pays before you invest more.
