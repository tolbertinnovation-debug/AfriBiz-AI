## Your phone is your shop window
id: marketing-01
topics: marketing, whatsapp, status, catalog, advertise

Most of your customers are already on WhatsApp. Use it:

• *WhatsApp Status*: post new stock, offers and happy customers (with permission) two or three times a week.
• *WhatsApp Business catalog*: list your main items with prices so people can order without calling.
• *Broadcast lists*: send a weekly message to regular customers ("Fresh tomatoes arrived today!").

Keep it useful. One good offer a week beats ten messages a day.

**Try this:** Post one photo of your best item on your Status today with the price.

**Quiz:** Which Status post is likely to bring a sale?
- A) A clear photo of fresh stock with the price and "order on WhatsApp" *
- B) "Business is open"
- C) A long message about your problems
**Why:** Customers act when they see the item, the price, and an easy way to order.

## Keep the customers you have
id: marketing-02
topics: loyalty, customer service, repeat

Getting a new customer costs much more than keeping an old one. Small things bring people back: greeting them by name, keeping their favourite items in stock, honest measures, and fixing mistakes quickly.

Try a simple loyalty card: after 10 purchases, a small gift. Record customers' names on sales and I can show you who your best customers are.

**Try this:** Thank your three most regular customers this week, and ask what else they would like you to stock.

**Quiz:** Why ask regular customers what they would like you to stock?
- A) It wastes time
- B) They will tell you items they now buy elsewhere *
- C) To make them feel bad
**Why:** Every item they buy from another shop is a sale you could have made.
