## How lenders look at a small business
id: credit-profile-01
triggers: credit_low
topics: credit score, lender, loan, profile, score

Without a payslip or collateral, lenders look at your *cash flow*: how much money comes in, how regularly, from how many customers, and whether you repaid earlier loans.

Your mobile money history is evidence of all of this. Forward your M-PESA / MoMo / Airtel messages to me (or import a statement) and send *score* to see your AfriBiz credit profile and what to improve.

You decide who sees it. Nothing is shared with a lender unless you say so.

**Try this:** Forward your last 10 mobile money messages, then send *score*.

**Quiz:** Which helps your credit profile the most?
- A) Taking payments in cash only
- B) Having customers pay by mobile money, regularly, from many different people *
- C) Keeping the balance at zero
**Why:** Regular, varied, recorded income is the strongest signal a lender can see.

## Small steps that raise your score
id: credit-profile-02
triggers: credit_low
topics: improve score

• Ask customers to pay by mobile money (show your till/number clearly).
• Keep a small balance on your account; don't empty it every evening.
• Repay any digital loan on or before the due date.
• Use one line for the business so the history is clean.
• Keep recording sales and expenses daily here.

Each of these moves one of the factors you see under *score*.

**Try this:** Put your mobile money number or till on a sign at your counter today.
