# Module: Managing stock
order: 5

## Never run out of your best sellers
id: stock-01
triggers: low_stock
topics: stock, reorder, inventory, restock

An empty shelf sends your customer to the next shop, and they may not come back. For each important item, set a *reorder level*: the quantity at which you buy more.

A simple rule: reorder level = what you sell per day × days it takes to restock + a little extra. If you sell 10 loaves a day and the supplier needs 2 days, reorder when you have about 25 left.

Tell me "reorder bread at 25" and I will warn you when you get there.

**Try this:** Set reorder levels for your top five items.

**Quiz:** You sell 6 crates a day and restocking takes 3 days. What is a good reorder level?
- A) 6
- B) 100
- C) About 20 *
**Why:** 6 × 3 = 18, plus a small safety margin, is about 20.

## First in, first out
id: stock-02
triggers: low_stock
topics: fifo, expiry, spoilage, waste

Sell old stock before new stock. Put new deliveries at the back and move older items to the front. This matters most for food, drinks, medicine and cosmetics with expiry dates.

Spoilt and expired goods are pure loss, so you paid for them and got nothing. Check dates every week and push items close to expiry with a small offer.

**Try this:** Rearrange one shelf today so the oldest stock is in front.

**Quiz:** A new delivery of milk arrives. Where should it go?
- A) In front, so it looks fresh
- B) Behind the older milk *
- C) It doesn't matter
**Why:** Older stock must sell first, or it expires on the shelf.

## Count your stock to find leaks
id: stock-03
triggers: low_stock
topics: count, shrinkage, theft, stocktake

Once a week, count what is on the shelf for your most valuable items and tell me: "count sugar 12". I will compare it with what should be there.

A gap means stock is leaking through theft, damage, family use or unrecorded sales. Even a small leak on a fast-moving item costs a lot over a year.

**Try this:** Count your top three items this week and send the counts.

**Quiz:** Records say 20 bags of sugar should be left, but you count 17. What should you do first?
- A) Ignore it, it's small
- B) Buy 3 more bags
- C) Check for unrecorded sales or damage, and watch that item closely *
**Why:** Small gaps grow. Find the cause before it becomes a habit.

## Fast movers and slow movers
id: stock-04
topics: slow moving, fast moving, 80/20

In most shops, a few items bring most of the profit. Look at *report month*: the top items list shows what really earns for you.

Keep fast movers always available. For slow movers, buy smaller quantities, and don't let money sit on the shelf for months. Money in slow stock is money that can't buy the items customers want.

**Try this:** Name one slow item you will stop restocking, and put that money into a fast seller.

**Quiz:** Which is the better use of 5,000?
- A) Stock that sells in 3 days with a 15% margin *
- B) Stock that sells in 3 months with a 30% margin
- C) Both the same
**Why:** Fast stock can be sold and rebought many times; 15% earned 20+ times beats 30% earned once.
